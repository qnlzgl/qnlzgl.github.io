PaceNow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

In short: PaceNow does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app uses is processed on your iPhone while you run.

What the app accesses

  • Location (GPS). Used to measure your speed and distance and to calibrate your pace. It is used while you run, and while a run is in progress the app keeps using it with the screen off so your run continues to be tracked. Allowing Precise Location gives the best accuracy.
  • Motion & Fitness (accelerometer, gyroscope, step counter). Used to detect your steps and estimate your pace and distance.

What happens to your data

  • Location and motion data are processed in real time on your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app does not save your runs. When you finish a run, the numbers are discarded when you tap Done.
  • The only things stored on your device are your preferences (distance unit and pace refresh rate).

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Your choices

You can change or revoke the Location and Motion & Fitness permissions at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security or Settings > PaceNow. Without them the app cannot calculate your pace.

Children

PaceNow is not directed to children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if the app ever adds features that store or send data), this page and its date will be updated before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/qnlzgl/qnlzgl.github.io/issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.
