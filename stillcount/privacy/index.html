Stillcount Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

In short: Stillcount does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • The habits you create (type, name, quit date, daily goal, and optional cost, time and usage estimates).
  • Check-ins (mood, amount and optional notes), slips, and your list of reasons.
  • App settings (language, theme, reminder time, currency symbol, Face ID lock).

This data is stored in the app's local storage on your iPhone and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Delete all data, removes it. You can export it at any time with Settings › Export my data. Copies in your own iCloud or iPhone backups are handled by Apple under your Apple settings.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs, and no network requests with your data.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Notifications

Reminders and milestone notifications are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings, and you can choose discreet notification text so habit names never appear on your lock screen.

Face ID / passcode lock

If you turn on the lock, the app asks iOS to verify you. Stillcount never receives or stores biometric data.

Children

Stillcount is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Health disclaimer

Stillcount is a self-tracking tool, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. If you may be dependent on alcohol or another substance, speak to a doctor before stopping suddenly.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if the app ever adds features that send data), this page and its date will be updated before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/qnlzgl/qnlzgl.github.io/issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.

Stillcount developer · Guanlun Zhao

静数隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

简而言之：静数不收集、不上传、不共享、不出售任何个人数据。你输入的一切都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。

保存在你设备上的内容

  • 你创建的习惯（类型、名称、戒断时间、每日目标，以及可选的花费、时间和用量估算）。
  • 打卡记录（心情、完成量和可选备注）、破戒记录，以及你写下的理由。
  • App 设置（语言、主题、提醒时间、货币符号、面容 ID 锁）。

这些数据保存在 iPhone 上的 App 本地存储中，不会发送给我们或任何人。删除 App，或使用「设置 › 删除全部数据」即可清除。你可以随时通过「设置 › 导出我的数据」导出。你自己的 iCloud 或 iPhone 备份中的副本，由 Apple 按你的 Apple 设置处理。

我们不做的事

  • 无需账号，无需登录。
  • 没有统计分析、广告、崩溃上报或追踪。
  • 没有第三方 SDK，不会通过网络发送你的数据。
  • 不向第三方出售或共享任何数据。

通知

提醒和里程碑通知都是在 iPhone 本地安排的通知，不经过任何推送服务器。你可以在 App 内或 iOS 设置中关闭，也可以选择「隐蔽通知」，锁屏上不会显示习惯名称。

面容 ID / 密码锁

开启锁定后，App 会请求 iOS 验证你的身份。静数不会接收或保存任何生物识别数据。

儿童

静数不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

健康声明

静数是自我记录工具，不是医疗器械，不提供医疗建议。如果你可能对酒精或其他物质有依赖，请在突然停止前咨询医生。

变更

如本政策有变更（例如 App 将来增加会发送数据的功能），本页及生效日期会在变更生效前更新。

联系

对本政策有疑问？请在 github.com/qnlzgl/qnlzgl.github.io/issues 提交问题。问题是公开可见的，请不要填写个人信息。

静数开发者 · Guanlun Zhao
